Our story

Three decades of standing beside our clients

Founded in 1994 by Jonathan Pierce, Lexoria began as a two-attorney practice with a simple conviction: every client deserves counsel that treats their matter as if it were our own.

Today our team of fourteen attorneys serves individuals, families and businesses across Ontario — in the boardroom, at the negotiating table, and before every level of court.
